Meet the founder

“When I was learning to code, it was hard to find beginner-friendly resources that didn't cost money or require traveling.”

Hi! I'm Harper Steenstra, a high school student at Morse High School. I fell in love with coding in third grade, dragging colorful blocks across the screen. When that class ended, there was nowhere else to learn.

So I built Code For ME: everything a beginner needs, all in one place, right here in Maine.
